Privacy Policy - Marchtide Uploader

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Marchtide Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Marchtide YouTube channels to upload videos to those channels.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google OAuth to upload videos (youtube.upload scope) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly scope) for the owner's own channels.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos and read upload status. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
